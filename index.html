About me

How I design

I'm Diego Muñoz, a game designer from Valencia, Spain, studying Game and Interactive Media Design at Florida Universitària.

As a generalist I understand development from a global perspective, but my real passion is designing encounters, systems and levels. I have an analytical, problem-solving mindset: I like to document, measure and balance until the experience clicks.

I'm looking for my first professional opportunity at a studio where I can bring my vision, keep growing in the craft and help create memorable games alongside a collaborative team.

My 3 design pillars

01

Combat

Dynamic, unique encounters with pacing, tension and clear decisions.

I design enemies and encounters around readable patterns, build tension and release across each fight, and make sure every player decision has a visible consequence.

02

Systems

The underlying logic and math: economies, difficulty curves and balancing.

I model economies, progression and difficulty in spreadsheets, test them against real numbers and iterate until every resource and reward carries weight.

03

Level Design

Greyboxing and blockouts of spaces that guide the player fluidly.

I use layout, sightlines and landmarks to orient the player without signposting, and control the pacing between exploration and action.
