Overview

Lobstar is a pixel-art arcade beat 'em up inspired by classic 1990s arcade titles. You control a monstrous kaiju destroying iconic cities around the world.

The monster grows in size and power, unlocks new areas of the map and faces ever stronger enemies — police, tanks, helicopters and aircraft. The design focus: fast pacing, large-scale destruction, a retro identity and replayability.
