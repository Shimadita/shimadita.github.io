🏆 Winner · Florida Game Fest 2026

Felp Wanted

VR psychological horror: inspect documents from an access-control booth and decide who gets in — and who isn't human.

My roleGame Designer · NPCs & documents
PlatformVirtual reality (seated)
GenreHorror · Document inspection
AwardFlorida Game Fest 2026
01

Overview

You're a former plant worker left in a wheelchair after a workplace accident. To avoid a lawsuit, the company has shut you away in the access-control booth of a retro-futuristic oil plant. With a card reader, a 90s-style touchscreen computer, a CCTV camera and two stamps, you decide whether whoever reaches the window is who they claim to be — or a corrupted entity trying to get in.

The experience is designed to be played seated, matching the protagonist's point of view. The demo is a 10-minute vertical slice built for the Florida Game Fest.

03

NPC interaction

Every visit follows the same rhythm so the player internalises it — what changes is who is on the other side of the glass:

1 · Reaches the windowA floating speech bubble with a greeting and small talk about the shift, the pipes or the cold corridor.
2 · Hands over the papersPicking them up advances the dialogue.
3 · Reacts to the stampLeaves thankful or angry. If it was an impostor and you rejected it, the jumpscare hits.
  • Random visitors: bear, cow, pig and other anthropomorphic animals (good in the demo); clown and goat (bad).
  • Animation set: idle, walk, hand over documents, talk and jumpscare (rejected bad ones only).
04

Documents & forgeries

Random visitors' documents are generated procedurally from a clear set of rules:

ACCESS CARDTRUS

EXP.14/09/20XX
SECTORB-2

Valid: correct name and an expiry date after today (22/05/20XX).
ACCESS CARDTRUS
🐐
NAMEGuillemo Algodón
NºTRUS-412-306-918
EXP.19/05/20XX
SECTORB-2
DENIED
Forged: a subtle typo in the name, and expired a few days ago.
  • Two types: an access card for internal workers (name, photo, expiry and sector) and an ID for outsiders (name, photo, expiry and number).
  • Number format: CODE-XXX-XXX-XXX — a factory code (LABU, TRUS, PLAY, MATT) plus 9 random digits.
  • Names: 50 valid textile-themed names (Josefina Estambre, Margarita Pelusa…) and 50 forged versions with subtle typos (Jozefina Estambre, Margarita Peluza…).
  • Expiry: good visitors get a date after the current day; bad ones get a recently expired date.
  • Discrepancies: differences between the 3D model and the photo, corroded or inhuman features, or absurd papers like cardboard or flyers.
05

Key characters

Three scripted events pace the demo, and each one tests a different player skill:

10:00

Eustaquio

Impostor

Pushy: asks twice and bangs on the glass if rejected. Hands over flyers instead of credentials. Let him in and the next visitor brings a blackout; reject him and he jumpscares.

11:00

Alfred

Good · pufferfish janitor

Friendly, jokes about puffing up from stress and humidity. In the full game his mood drops if you let too many impostors in.

11:40

Damien

The anomaly

Silent, with seemingly valid papers. Breaks the fourth wall: stares into the CCTV camera and, when you look away from it, stares at you. Whatever you choose, static lingers until the end of the shift.
